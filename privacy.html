ClipFarm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

ClipFarm is a private, single-user publishing tool operated by Josh (the "operator") to post his own short-form videos to his own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

What data it accesses

Only through official APIs, and only with the permissions the operator grants:

  • the operator's own basic profile (display name, avatar, follower or subscriber count);
  • the operator's own videos and their statistics (views, likes, comment counts);
  • comments posted by other people on the operator's own videos;
  • direct messages sent to the operator's own Instagram account.

How it is used

  • To publish the operator's own videos at scheduled times.
  • To show the operator his own analytics in private reports.
  • To hide spam and abusive comments on the operator's own posts, answer common questions (such as crediting the original creator), and draft replies for the operator to approve.

ClipFarm never likes, follows, messages or comments on any other account.

Where data goes

  • Comments and messages written by other people are processed only on the operator's own computer and are not sent to any AI service.
  • Still frames of the operator's own videos may be sent to Google's Gemini API for an automated quality check.
  • Private notifications to the operator are delivered through Telegram.
  • Data is never sold, never shared for advertising, and never used by ClipFarm to train AI models.

Storage, retention and deletion

Data is stored in a local database on the operator's own computer. Access tokens are stored locally in a file readable only by the ClipFarm system account. Data is kept only as long as the operator needs it for his own reporting. Anyone whose comment or message was processed can ask for its deletion at the contact below, and it will be deleted within 7 days.

You can revoke ClipFarm's access at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions
  • Instagram: Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites

YouTube API Services

ClipFarm uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with ClipFarm's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). See also the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
